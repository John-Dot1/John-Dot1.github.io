02 - Selected work

What I built this year

Three projects from first year. Each one lists the tools, what I was responsible for, and what I would do differently next time.

Operation Hawthorn

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Team · 8 weeks

A third-person, single-player stealth-action game built entirely with Blueprints. Players progress through three missions, culminating in a final bomb-planting objective.

My role: I was responsible for the gameplay development, including player mechanics, enemy AI, Animation Blueprints and HUD implementation, as well as designing the three levels. If I were to revisit the project, I would spend more time refining the level layouts and recording a wider range of sounds.

Ward Zero

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 8 weeks

A first-person, single-player survival horror game set in a purgatory-like underground hospital housing unknown horrors. The player must explore the hospital and find a way to escape.

My role: Ward Zero was a solo project, so I created the entire game, including its gameplay systems, environments, HUD and level design. If I were to make it again, I would put more work into the materials, particularly normal maps, to add depth to the environment and improve the flat textures.

The Nut Cracker

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Solo · 5 weeks

The Nut Cracker is a Christmas-themed 2D platformer where players take on the role of Santa, head-stomping evil Nutcrackers who have set out to destroy Christmas.

My role: I created the entire game independently, from the gameplay mechanics and Blueprints to the sprite artwork, HUD and level design. If I were to revisit the project, I would expand it with more levels, enemies and mechanics. I was surprised by how much fun the finished game was and would like to take the idea further.

03 - About

About me

I am a second-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I have always been passionate about games but I wanted to learn more about how they are actually made and develop the skills to create my own.

I am most interested in gameplay programming and creating the systems that control how a game plays and feels. This year I have worked mainly with Unreal Engine and Blueprints, alongside modelling and texturing my own assets and recording and implementing audio.

Next, I want to learn C++ and continue improving my skills with Unreal Engine. I also want to work on a longer team project and build more complex gameplay systems.
