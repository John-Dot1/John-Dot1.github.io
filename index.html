Project Hawthorn

Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Team · 8 weeks

A third-person stealth-action game built entirely with Blueprints. Players progress through three missions, culminating in a final bomb-planting objective.

My role: Developed all gameplay systems, responsible for the complete game, including gameplay logic, animation Blueprints, HUD implementation and level design. If I were to do this project again I would record more sounds and work more on the level layouts.

03 - About

About me

I am a first-year Games Development student at Belfast Metropolitan College, Castlereagh campus. I have always been passionate about games but I wanted to learn more about how they are actually made and develop the skills to create my own.

I am most interested in gameplay programming and creating the systems that control how a game plays and feels. This year I have worked mainly with Unreal Engine and Blueprints, alongside modelling and texturing my own assets and recording and implementing audio.

Next, I want to learn C++ and continue improving my skills with Unreal Engine. I also want to work on a longer team project and build more complex gameplay systems.
